About Me

I’m Adel Marcano, a CompTIA Security+ certified cybersecurity senior at Old Dominion University. I work at the intersection of IT operations and security, with hands-on experience supporting users, configuring systems, and validating security controls across Windows and Linux environments.

I’m a fundamentals-first builder. I like finding what’s actually happening, fixing the root cause, and proving the fix worked. That shows up in my security work (vulnerability assessment, hardening, and verification) and in my development work (deploying real apps, setting up authentication, and enforcing least-privilege access rules).

I also care a lot about clarity—clean UI, readable systems, and documentation that someone else can follow. Whether I’m troubleshooting, writing security rules, or shipping an early-access build, my goal is predictable behavior, secure defaults, and steady improvement over time.

I’m currently pursuing entry-level IT, SOC, or security operations roles where I can keep learning while contributing to real-world security and support work.
